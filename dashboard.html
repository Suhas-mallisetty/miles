New Hampshire shopping trip

NH has no sales tax, so an in-store price there beats the same online price shipped to a taxed state. Take this page on your phone and log anything you see with python3 tracker.py log --new ITEM OPTION "Store, Town" PRICE.

Massagers (neck pillow, foot, knee)
  • Best Buy (Nashua, Salem, Manchester, Concord, Newington). Carries the Nekteck neck massager online ($59.99). Check "pick up today".
  • Target / Walmart (most NH towns). Check RENPHO, HoMedics and Nekteck stock in the app before you drive.
  • TJ Maxx / Marshalls / HomeGoods. Often have RENPHO and HoMedics massagers well below list price. Stock is hit or miss.
  • Kohl's. Sells RENPHO and HoMedics massagers, and on Black Friday adds Kohl's Cash on top of the sale.
  • Costco (Nashua). Holiday massager bundles appear in Nov–Dec.
Swim goggles
  • Dick's Sporting Goods (Nashua, Salem, Manchester, Newington). Speedo and TYR fitness goggles. Try them on: press each eyecup lightly to your face without the strap. A good fit holds suction for a second.
  • Outlet malls: Merrimack Premium Outlets (Merrimack), Tanger (Tilton), Settlers Green (North Conway, worth it if you're going north), Kittery, ME (just over the bridge from Portsmouth). Look at Nike, Under Armour and sports outlets for swim gear. Massager brands rarely have outlet stores.
  • Alternatives to try on if the Kayenne doesn't fit: TYR Special Ops 2.0 (non-polarized, since polarized is too dark indoors), Speedo Hydro Comfort, Aqua Sphere Seal 3 (mask-style, most comfortable for long swims).

Store stock changes daily. Call or check each store's app before driving.
